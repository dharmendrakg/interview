# 00 — Job Search Strategy, Pipeline & Compensation Playbook

Interview prep without a deliberate sourcing, referral, and leveling strategy leaves offers to luck. At **11+ YOE**, treat your search like a high-leverage engineering funnel: **warm referrals > direct HM outreach > curated cold applications**.

---

## 1. Target Roles, Markets & Level Calibration

### Primary Role Targets (Strictly Individual Contributor — No People Management)
1. **Staff / Principal Frontend Engineer (IC)** (Design systems, Next.js/React 19 architecture, complex telemetry UIs, Web Vitals, accessibility)
2. **Staff / Senior Full-Stack Engineer (Frontend-Leaning · IC, L5/L6)** (End-to-end product architecture, Node/Postgres forensics, migrations, React/TS)
3. **AI-Native Product / Staff Engineer (IC)** (MCP tooling, trace-aware review automation, structured-output LLM features, verification harnesses)

> **IC-Only Rule (Filter Out EM / TLM Tracks):** At 11+ YOE, recruiters frequently pitch Engineering Manager or Tech Lead Manager roles. Make it explicit on screen #1 that you are targeting **hands-on IC Staff/Senior engineering**—not direct-report management.
> Crisp answer: *"I want to remain a hands-on Individual Contributor. I multiply team velocity through system architecture, design systems, high-bar code reviews, production forensics, and developer/AI tooling (like my MCP + Zipkin server)—not people management."*

### Target Market & Company Tiers (Build 25–30 Targets)
Split your list so you **never burn dream companies while warming up**:

| Tier | Profile | When to Apply | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 2 (Calibration — Apply Week 1)** | Strong Series B+ startups, well-funded SaaS/devtools, remote EU/US EOR roles, solid India product firms | **Week 1** (5–8 roles) | Test resume conversion, calibrate 90s pitch, shake off interview rust in real loops |
| **Tier 1 (Dream Targets — Apply Weeks 2–3+)** | Top product companies / GCCs (e.g., Stripe, Atlassian, Rippling, Uber, Razorpay, Datadog, Microsoft, top AI-native startups) | **After** mock rubric `≥ 4/5` & Tier-2 screens | Maximize L6 / Staff offer odds & batch finals within a 2-week window for leverage |
| **Tier 3 (Baseline Safety)** | Stable enterprise/product roles matching your exact React/Node/MUI stack | **Week 2+** (3–5 roles) | Ensure offer optionality & comp floor |

## 2. Warm Referral & Direct Outreach Engine

At 11+ YOE, cold portal submissions have low signal-to-noise. Prioritise warm paths for **every Tier-1 and Tier-2 target**:

1. **Alumni & Past-Colleague Graph (Mine First):**
   - **NIT Trichy MCA alumni** (2012–2015 + adjacent batches) in Staff/EM/Principal roles.
   - **Ex-NortonLifeLock / Symantec colleagues** (2015–2020) now spread across Tier-1 Big Tech, fintech, and security startups.
   - **Ex-Comcast engineers/managers** who moved to target Bengaluru or remote orgs.
2. **Direct Hiring Manager / Staff Peer Outreach (LinkedIn / Email):**
   Send after applying or instead of waiting in an ATS queue. Keep it **4 lines max**:
   > *"Hi [Name] — I'm a hands-on Staff/Senior Full-Stack Engineer (11+ yrs IC, ex-NortonLifeLock, currently UI Lead (IC) on Comcast's Salesforce-to-React/Node Sphere platform). I saw your team is hiring for [Role] and noticed you're scaling [specific product/architecture challenge]. Recently shipped an MCP server linking PR diffs + Zipkin traces for AI code review, drove 4 Salesforce domain migrations, and solved a 16× Postgres query bottleneck. Would love to share my resume if helpful—happy to connect for 10 mins."*
3. **Warm Referral Request Template:**
   > *"Hey [Name], hope you're doing well! I'm exploring hands-on IC Staff/Senior Full-Stack or Staff Frontend roles and spotted [Role + Req ID/URL] at [Company]. With 11+ yrs across React/Next.js/Node/Postgres (6 yrs driving UI architecture & migrations at Comcast Sphere + 5 yrs at NortonLifeLock), it's a close fit. Happy to send a tailored 3-bullet blurb + resume so submitting a referral takes 60 seconds—would you be open to referring me?"*

## 3. Public Proof & Digital Footprint Sync (Complete in Days 1–3)

Before sending outreach, align what every recruiter/HM clicks from `resume.md`:
- [ ] **LinkedIn Sync:** Update Headline & About to match the **IC Staff/Senior** positioning in `resume.md` (explicitly state *Individual Contributor* and surface UI architecture, Design Systems, Postgres forensics, Salesforce CDC migration, MCP/Zipkin AI tooling—avoid "Manager" or ambiguous "Lead" titles that trigger EM outreach).
- [ ] **GitHub Audit:** Pin 3 high-signal repositories with clean READMEs and architecture diagrams (e.g., an anonymized/OSS MCP trace-review pattern or DevFlow pipeline engine, plus clean TS/React/Next.js code).
- [ ] **Live Product Sanity Check:** Verify `wtool.dev` and `faucetworld.in` load cleanly on desktop/mobile with crisp landing copy (interviewers *will* click your links).
- [ ] **Publish 1–2 High-Signal Technical Write-Ups (Week 1–2):**
  1. *"How a 63 KB Postgres TOAST Column + Retry Loop Caused Production OOMs (and How We Found It)"*
  2. *"Connecting Zipkin Distributed Traces to AI Code Review via MCP"*
  *(Post on LinkedIn + personal/dev blog—creates inbound Staff/AI-engineer recruiter flow and validates claims before interviews.)*

## 4. Compensation Calibration & Recruiter Screen Rules

### Pre-Screen Prep
- Research exact 2026 L5 (Senior) vs. L6 (Staff) total compensation bands for **Bengaluru Tier-1 product companies** (Levels.fyi / AmbitionBox / peers) and **remote US/EU roles** (USD contractor/EOR bands) *before* your first recruiter call.
- Set three explicit numbers privately: **Walk-away floor**, **Realistic L5/Senior target**, and **Staff/L6 target**.

### Handling Premature Comp Questions (India & Remote Screens)
- **If asked "What is your current/expected CTC?" on Call #1:**
  > *"I'm still early in conversations and want to make sure the role scope and level (Senior vs. Staff) align first. Based on 11+ years leading UI architecture, backend forensics, and AI engineering workflows, I'm targeting market-competitive Staff/Senior total compensation for this band—what is the budgeted range for this level?"*
- **If forced to give a number (automated form or insistent recruiter):** Give a **research-backed market range** anchored at the top half of L6/Staff (never anchor on legacy base salary alone).
- **Clarify Coding Format Upfront:** Ask every recruiter:
  > *"Could you share the format for the technical rounds—specifically whether coding rounds are algorithmic DSA, practical React/TS machine coding, or PR review, and whether AI coding assistants are permitted or disabled?"*
